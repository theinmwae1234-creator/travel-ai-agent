Tokyo is famous for anime culture, Akihabara, modern technology, sushi, ramen, and shopping districts such as Shibuya and Harajuku.

Tokyo attractions include Sensoji Temple, Tokyo Skytree, Akihabara, Meiji Shrine, and Tsukiji Outer Market.

Singapore is known for Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa Island, hawker centres, Chinatown, and Orchard Road.

Singapore food experiences include Maxwell Food Centre, Lau Pa Sat, and Newton Food Centre.

Paris is known for the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and Montmartre.

Paris food experiences include French bakeries, cafés, Michelin-starred restaurants, and local bistros.
